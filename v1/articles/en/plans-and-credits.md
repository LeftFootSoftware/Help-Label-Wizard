## Label Wizard pricing: plans, label credits, cancelling and refunds

Label Wizard: Barcode Labels is a Shopify app by Left Foot Software. It has two plans: Start Free ($0 a month plus usage: 20 free labels every 30 days, then $0.03 per label, falling to $0.01 at higher volumes) and Unlimited ($24.99 a month or $199 a year, no per-label fee). Label Wizard takes no commission or share of your sales.

Prices are in US dollars. Shopify bills monthly prices and usage charges every 30 days, and the yearly price once a year. If your Shopify bill is in a local currency, Shopify converts the charges from US dollars at a rate that includes a 1.5% currency exchange fee.

This article covers only this Shopify app, not other products called Label Wizard.

Prices checked: 21 November 2026. The [Label Wizard listing in the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/label-wizard) always shows the current prices.

### The two plans

| Plan | Price per 30 days | Yearly | Labels included | Per-label price | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Start Free** | $0, plus usage | Not offered | 20 free every 30 days | $0.03 to $0.01 after the first 20 | Free setup help: chat, email and Zoom |
| **Unlimited** | $24.99 | $199 (save 34%) | Unlimited | None | Priority chat, email and Zoom |

Start Free charges per label. After the first 20 labels in each 30-day cycle, you pay for each label you print. Unlimited has no per-label fee.

Both plans have the same designer, label sizes and printing. There is no limit on designs or sheet sizes on either plan. You can save as many designs (templates) as you want on Start Free and on Unlimited.

### What uses label credits

Each label the app generates for printing uses one label credit. Credits count labels, not products or designs.

For example, if you print 3 labels for one product and 2 for another in the same run, that run uses 5 credits.

These do not use credits:

- Creating, editing and saving designs
- Previewing labels in the editor or in the sheet picker
- Opening the **Select a sheet or roll to print** window and choosing **Cancel**

### What Start Free costs

After the first 20 labels in each 30-day cycle, each label costs less as you print more:

- First 20 labels: free
- Labels 21 to 500: $0.03 each
- Labels 501 to 1,000: $0.02 each
- Labels 1,001 and more: $0.01 each

Each price applies only to the labels inside its band. Some examples:

- 100 labels: $2.40
- 300 labels: $8.40
- 500 labels: $14.40
- 1,000 labels: $24.40

Usage charges appear on your Shopify bill. Prices are in US dollars.

### What happens after my 20 free labels?

You keep printing. Label Wizard does not stop you at 20 labels; the listing says Start Free has "no usage cap". From label 21 in each 30-day cycle, each label costs $0.03, and less above 500 labels. The charges appear on your next Shopify bill.

### When does Unlimited cost less?

Unlimited costs less when you print more than about 1,060 labels every 30 days. On Start Free, 1,059 labels cost $24.99, the same as Unlimited's monthly price. Each label after that adds $0.01 on Start Free and nothing on Unlimited.

If you pay for Unlimited yearly ($199, about $16.36 per 30 days), Unlimited costs less from about 600 labels every 30 days.

### Is there a free trial?

There is no free trial. Start Free is $0 a month, and your first 20 labels every 30 days are free, so you can try Label Wizard on it.

### Check your plan and credits

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find **Account Information**. It shows your plan and billing.

While you design or print, the top of the page shows **Remaining Credits**.

### Change your plan

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Account Information**, choose **Change plan**.
3. On **Select a plan**, choose **Pay monthly** or **Pay yearly**.
4. Choose the plan you want, then approve it.

### How do I cancel?

To stop paying for Unlimited, change to Start Free or uninstall the app. Start Free has no monthly fee, so you pay only for the labels you print after the first 20.

To change to Start Free, follow the steps in **Change your plan** and choose **Start Free**. The app can show this plan as **Pay As You Go**.

To uninstall Label Wizard:

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** > **Apps**.
2. Next to Label Wizard, open the menu and choose **Uninstall**.
3. Choose **Uninstall** again to confirm.

Shopify says that uninstalling a paid app cancels future recurring charges, but you might still be billed for the current billing cycle. Per-label charges from the current cycle still appear on your next Shopify bill. Read more in Shopify's guide to [uninstalling apps](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/apps/uninstalling-apps).

### Can I get a refund?

Shopify bills Label Wizard's charges. To ask for a refund, choose **Chat with us** on the Label Wizard Dashboard. We review each request.

Shopify advises you to contact the app developer about a refund before you uninstall the app.

### If something goes wrong

- **You expected to pay nothing:** the first 20 labels in each 30-day cycle are free. Labels after that are charged per label on Start Free.
- **Your bill shows a usage charge you do not recognise:** count the labels you printed in that cycle. Each label the app generates is one credit, including labels you print again.
- **Your Shopify bill says "Pay As You Go":** that is the Start Free plan. Shopify invoices use the name Pay As You Go.
- **You have a billing question:** choose **Chat with us** on the Label Wizard Dashboard.

### Older plans (no longer offered)

Label Wizard sold these plans in the past.

- **Free:** $0. It ended on 20 July 2026, when Start Free (first called Pay As You Go) replaced it.
- **Standard:** charged at $4.99 or at $49.00. The last store moved onto it in May 2026.
- **Pro:** $14.99 per 30 days, or $150 per year, until September 2026. Pro then became today's Unlimited plan at $24.99, and some screens still show the name Pro.

If your app shows an older plan name, your price is the one on your Shopify bill. To move to a current plan, choose **Change plan**.
