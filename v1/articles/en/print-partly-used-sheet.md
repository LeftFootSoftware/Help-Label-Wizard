## Printing on a partly used label sheet

Use up a label sheet you already printed on: mark the positions that are gone, and Label Wizard prints only onto the free ones.

### Before you start

- A label sheet with some positions already used, and the same sheet chosen for your design.
- Products selected on **Print labels**. See [Printing labels for your products](/category/printing/printing-labels).

### Skip the used positions

1. On **Print labels**, choose **Print N labels**. The **Select a sheet or roll to print** window opens.
2. Check that **Sheet or roll** shows the sheet you are loading.
3. In the **Layout** preview, click each position that is already used on your sheet.
4. Check the counter under the preview, for example **2 positions skipped on the first sheet**.
5. Load the sheet into your printer the same way up as last time.
6. Choose **Print N labels**.

![screenshot:Layout preview of a 24-label sheet with the first two positions marked as skipped, the hint "Click positions already used on your first sheet to leave them blank." and the counter "2 positions skipped on the first sheet" with a Clear link](PartlyUsedSheetSkipPositions.png)

### What happens

The skipped positions stay blank on the PDF, and your labels fill the free positions in order. Skipping applies to the first sheet of the run only.

Skipped positions are not labels, so they do not count toward your plan. The button keeps showing the number of labels you print.

### Undo a skip

Choose **Clear** next to the counter. Every position is free again.

### If something goes wrong

- **Labels land on used positions:** check the counter before you print, and that the sheet faces the same way as in your last run.
- **Labels are shifted on the sheet:** set scale to 100% and margins to None in the print dialog. See [Getting the best print quality](/category/printing/print-quality-dpi).
- **Your sheet is not in the list:** its size does not match the design. See [Troubleshooting common printing problems](/category/account-troubleshooting/troubleshooting-printing).
