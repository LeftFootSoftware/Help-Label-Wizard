## Printing on thermal and continuous-feed printers

This guide covers printing Label Wizard designs on thermal and continuous-feed printers (Zebra, Rollo, DYMO-style, and similar).

### Pick roll or continuous stock

1. Design for a roll or **Continuous Feed** size that matches your physical labels (for example 4×6).
2. In Print labels, choose that same sheet/roll in the stock picker.
3. Set **Print resolution** to **203 DPI** unless your printer documentation recommends otherwise.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: ThermalPrintersStockPicker.png | Print modal showing Continuous Feed / roll selection and 203 DPI | source: harness -->

### Driver and printer setup

- Install the manufacturer’s driver or print utility.
- Set the printer’s label size to match the design.
- Disable options that resize the page to letter/A4 when you intend to print a single label.

### Browser printing to a thermal printer

Some setups print through the browser to the thermal printer; others use a print utility that watches a PDF. Follow your printer’s recommended path, but keep Label Wizard’s DPI and stock selection correct either way.

### Continuous feed vs sheets

Continuous designs are not multi-label office sheets. Do not pick an Avery sheet template when you are loading a thermal roll — the preview and layout will not match the printer.
