## Set up a thermal or roll printer

Print on a roll in a thermal label printer: Label Wizard prints one label per page, sized to the label, at 203 DPI.

### Before you start

- A design at your roll's label size. Find the roll's part number in **Sheets and rolls**, for example Dymo 30334, and choose **Design a label**. See [Find your label sheet or roll (Avery, Online Labels, Dymo, Uline)](/category/sheets-rolls-templates/sheets-and-rolls).
- Your printer installed on your computer, with the roll loaded.

### Print on a roll

1. Open the design and choose **Print**, then select your products. See [Print barcode labels for your products and variants](/category/printing/printing-labels).
2. In **Select a sheet or roll to print**, set **Paper size** to **Continuous Feed**. **Sheet or roll** picks a roll at your design's size.
3. Set **Printer resolution (DPI)** to **203 DPI**.
4. Check **Sheet or roll details**: **Labels Per Sheet** is 1, and **Paper Size** matches the label.
5. Choose the print button. It shows the label count, for example **Print 1 label**.
6. In your browser's print dialog, choose your label printer and a paper size that matches the label.

### Make 203 DPI the default

In **Settings**, set **Default printer DPI** to **203 DPI**. The print window then opens at 203 DPI each time. In **When you print**, choose whether the app opens your browser's print dialog or downloads a PDF. See [Change your app settings](/category/sheets-rolls-templates/app-settings).

### Add a roll that isn't listed

In **Sheets and rolls**, choose **Add custom** and set **Sheet or roll** to **Continuous Feed**, then enter the label size. See [Add a label size that isn't listed](/category/sheets-rolls-templates/custom-label-template).

### Good to know

- Label Wizard prints a PDF through your browser. It does not send printer commands such as ZPL or EPL.
- Printer models and drivers have not been tested by Label Wizard. Print one label before a full roll.

### If something goes wrong

- **The hint says "This label is small, so it will print at 600 DPI":** the DPI is set to 300. Choose **203 DPI** for a thermal printer.
- **The label prints small on a large page, or across several labels:** the paper size in your printer's dialog does not match the label. Set it to the label size.
- **No roll is offered at your design's size:** add your roll with **Add custom**, as above.
