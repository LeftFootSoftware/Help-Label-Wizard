## Start from a ready-made design

Copy a ready-made design from the **Design gallery**, then change it to fit your products and your label stock.

### Before you start

- Know your label size. See [Find your label sheet or roll (Avery, Online Labels, Dymo, Uline)](/category/sheets-rolls-templates/sheets-and-rolls).

### Copy a design

1. In the app's menu, choose **Design gallery**. You can also choose **Browse ready-made designs** on the **Design a label** page.
2. Find a design at your label's size. Each card shows its name and size; switch units with **mm**, **cm** or **in**.
3. Choose **Use this design**.
4. In the **Variant** panel, choose a few of your products and check the canvas.
5. Change the text, fields and colours you need.
6. Choose **Save** in the **Unsaved changes** bar.

### What happens

**Use this design** creates your own copy straight away and opens it in the editor. The copy takes the design's name plus a number, for example "Apparel Hang Tag-(3)", and appears in **My designs**.

### Designs in the gallery

- **Shoebox Label:** 4.00 x 1.00 in
- **Barcode label:** 2.25 x 1.25 in
- **Product Label:** 3.00 x 2.00 in
- **Markdown Sticker:** 1.00 x 2.00 in, with a sale price
- **Product Details:** 3.00 x 3.00 in
- **Round product QR Code:** 2.00 in circle
- **Round Store QR sticker:** 1.50 in circle
- **Hangtag Sticker:** 1.75 x 2.00 in
- **Polybag Sticker:** 2.00 x 2.00 in
- **Apparel Hang Tag:** 2.00 x 3.50 in

The gallery previews use sample products. Your own products show once you open your copy.

### If something goes wrong

- **You have extra copies of a design:** each **Use this design** click makes a new one. In **My designs**, open the card's menu and choose **Delete design**. Deleting cannot be undone.
- **You want a clearer name:** in **My designs**, open the card's menu and choose **Rename design**. See [Rename, copy or delete a saved design](/category/designing-labels/manage-saved-designs).
- **No design matches your label size:** start from your sheet or roll instead. See [Create a label design](/category/designing-labels/create-label-design).
