## Using the Library of ready-made designs

This guide covers the Design gallery: browsing ready-made designs, previewing them with your products, and starting a label from a sample.

### Open the Design gallery

From the Dashboard choose **Browse ready-made designs**, or open **Design gallery** from navigation when available.

![screenshot:Design gallery grid of ready-made designs](DesignLibraryGallery.png)

### Browse designs

- Scroll or page through available samples.
- Look for layouts that match your stock size and the fields you need (barcode, price, title).

### Preview with your products

When the gallery offers sample data controls, use **Change variant** (or previous/next) to see how a design looks with your own catalog. This is the best way to judge whether titles fit and barcodes look correct.

![screenshot:Ready-made design preview with a store product variant](DesignLibraryPreviewVariant.png)

### Start from a ready-made design

1. Open the design you like.
2. Choose **Create label** / start design (wording may vary slightly).
3. Adjust text, fields, and branding in the editor.
4. **Save** under **My designs**.

Ready-made designs are starting points — customize them so they match your brand and stock.
