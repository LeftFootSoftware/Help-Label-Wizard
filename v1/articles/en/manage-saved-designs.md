## Rename, copy or delete a saved design

Rename a design, copy it to make a variation, or delete one you no longer need, from **My designs**.

### Before you start

- At least one saved design. See [Create a label design](/category/designing-labels/create-label-design).

### Find your designs

In the app's menu, choose **My designs**. Each card shows the design's name, its size, and **Print labels** and **Edit** buttons. If you have many designs, the page controls at the bottom show where you are, for example "1 of 5".

### Rename a design

1. On the design's card, open the menu.
2. Choose **Rename design**.
3. Type the new **Design name** and choose **Save**.

### Copy a design

1. On the design's card, open the menu.
2. Choose **Duplicate design**.
3. In **New design name**, keep the suggested name or type your own, then choose **Duplicate design**.
4. On the copy's card, choose **Edit** to change it.

Use a copy to try a new layout or a second label size without changing the design you print today.

### Delete a design

1. On the design's card, open the menu.
2. Choose **Delete design**.
3. In **Delete design?**, choose **Delete**.

![screenshot:Delete design? dialog: "This permanently deletes the design. This action cannot be undone.", Cancel and Delete](DeleteDesignDialog-v2.png)

Deleting cannot be undone. Your products and their barcodes in Shopify do not change.

### If something goes wrong

- **You have several designs with the same name and a number, such as "-(3)":** each **Use this design** click in the **Design gallery** makes a new copy. Delete the extras.
- **You are not sure which copy you print with:** rename them clearly, for example by label size or stock part number.
