## Printing labels for your products

This guide covers the **Print labels** page: search, filters, selecting products, and running a print.

### Open Print labels

Open **Print labels** from the navigation, from a saved design, or from Label Wizard actions on a product or variant page.

You will see your selected design summary and a **Products** table.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: PrintingLabelsPage.png | Print labels page with products table and Print button | source: harness -->

### Search and filters

- Search products by title or barcode.
- Filter by **Location** when you need inventory for a specific location.
- Use product type or other filters when shown.
- Clear filters with **Clear all** when you want the full list again.

If you arrived from a product or variant action, a product/variant chip may already narrow the list. Clear that chip to return to the wider catalog while keeping your design selected.

### Select products

1. Tick the rows you want to print.
2. Or use **Select all** for every product in the current filter (see [Printing labels for every product at once](/category/printing/print-all-products)).
3. Adjust quantities in the quantity column or with **Set Quantity**.

### Print

1. Choose **Print** from the page header or the bulk actions next to the selection.
2. In the print dialog, choose **Manufacturer**, **Paper size**, and **Sheet or roll** as needed.
3. Confirm **Print resolution** (DPI) if shown.
4. Review the **Label Preview**, then print.
